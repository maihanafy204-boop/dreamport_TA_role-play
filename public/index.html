Your first day as a Travel Consultant

In this short assessment you'll chat, in writing, with a customer. It takes about 10–15 minutes. Please use a computer if you can, and make sure you won't be interrupted.

Use the same email you applied with, so we can match your result.

Your situation

A lead from the follow-up queue

Ground rules

  • There are exactly two ticket options. You'll see them, with all details, in a reference panel next to the chat the whole time.
  • Listed prices are final. No discounts, price matching or negotiation on the ticket price.
  • Recommend the option that fits the customer's needs — you'll need to find out what those are. Add-ons are optional; offer them only if they're relevant.
  • Use only the facts in the panel. Don't invent fees, promotions, seats or policies.
  • You send the first message. Write as you would to a real customer — clear, professional, in your own words.
  • The chat ends when the customer decides, or after 15 minutes. Copy & paste is disabled.
